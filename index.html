About Max

Personal. Selective. Experienced.

Max Hamidi has spent over three decades in commercial real estate — from retail operations to loan packaging to investment advisory. He does not take every deal that comes through the door. He is selective because his clients deserve focused attention, not divided loyalty.

Based in Germantown, Tennessee, Max advises locally while staying connected to broader commercial relationships, referral partners, and the eXp Commercial network.
